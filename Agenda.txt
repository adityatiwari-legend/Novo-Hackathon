System management process 

- Sandeep described the end-to-end responsibilities of system managers, including vendor selection, risk assessment, regulatory evaluation, supplier assessment, and system implementation in manufacturing areas.
- Sandeep explained the initial assessment steps for implementing a new system, such as gathering user requirements, budgeting, and evaluating risks and regulatory impacts.
- Sandeep outlined the process of supplier assessment, including issuing RFPs or RFIs, evaluating suppliers based on business, risk, and technology criteria, and finalizing procurement before system design and development.
- Sandeep detailed the subsequent phases after supplier selection, including system design, development, pilot implementation, and comprehensive testing activities.
- Sandeep explained that each phase of system implementation and operation requires specific documentation, including change requests, implementation plans, testing records, maintenance logs, calibration reports, and stakeholder agreements.
- Sandeep described the challenge of locating and retrieving historical documentation across the entire system lifecycle to answer regulatory or business queries efficiently.
- Siddharth discussed the difficulties of managing and storing documentation, especially when transitioning from paper-based to digital solutions, and highlighted uncertainty about retention periods for different documents.
- Siddharth emphasized the importance of following standard procedures and guidelines to ensure consistent medicine quality and traceability of operational activities.
- Sandeep explained that agents can check document completeness, compliance with regulatory requirements, and retrieve information based on queries.
Audit readiness and compliance 

- Sandeep highlighted the importance of maintaining accurate and complete records to address regulatory investigations, such as patient complaints related to medicines produced years ago.
- Sandeep explained the challenge of locating and retrieving specific historical documentation to demonstrate compliance during regulatory audits.
- Sandeep described the need to conduct gap assessments and provide evidence to regulators that all manufacturing parameters were valid and processes followed correctly.
- Sandeep described the importance of retrieving accurate, evidence-based, and time-variant documentation for audit scenarios, emphasizing that agents must provide information from the correct document version based on the requested timeframe.
Hackathon data and resources 

- Sandeep explained that participants will receive synthetic data sets and documents for use as knowledge sources in their agent solutions.
- Sandeep described common challenges with AI solutions reading various data formats, such as images, tables in PDFs, and information in parentheses or footnotes.
Hackathon tools and templates 

- Sandeep confirmed that participants can use open source tools, as well as enterprise-level tools like ChatGPT, Copilot, and LangGraph, to build their agents.
- Sandeep explained that participants are encouraged to explore and use any new or open source tools available in the market for their prototype, not just current trending options.
- Sandeep clarified that participants will not receive credits for using external tools and should rely on freely available resources.
- Sandeep clarified that participants are encouraged to use their creativity in selecting AI tools and design approaches, including both new and older models, depending on available credits.
- Sandeep confirmed that participants can use UI design tools like Streamblade or Langgraph to build their solutions.
Prototype development guidance 

- Sandeep advised participants to build a simple prototype first and then iterate to add more features.
Prototype deployment requirements 

- Sandeep clarified that agents for the prototype should run locally on participants' machines, including during the final presentation.
Prototype testing process 

- Sandeep stated that the demo prototype will be tested with multiple scenarios, and participants will receive test scenarios to ensure proper responses and error-free operation.
Document version handling 

- Sandeep emphasized the importance of handling different date formats in documents from various regions, such as Denmark, India, and China, to select the correct document version.
Presentation expectations 

- Sandeep highlighted that the presentation should be simple, avoiding technical jargon, and should build trust with non-IT management audiences.
